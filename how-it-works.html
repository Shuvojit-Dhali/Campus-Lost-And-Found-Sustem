How CampusFind Works

Lost something on campus? Found an item that isn't yours? CampusFind connects lost items with their owners in a few simple steps — with smart AI-powered matching to speed things up.

Four Simple Steps

From report to recovery — here's the full journey.

1

Create an Account

Sign up with your full name, a unique Student ID, and a password. Log in to start reporting and browsing items.

2

Report an Item

Submit a lost or found report with the item's title, category, color, location, date, and an optional photo.

3

Get Smart Matches

CampusFind scans open reports in the same category and opposite type, then suggests possible matches on your dashboard.

4

Claim & Recover

The finder confirms the match, you request a claim, and the reporter approves it. Status changes to Claimed — item recovered!

📝 Reporting a Lost or Found Item

Whether you lost something or picked up an item that isn't yours, reporting is quick and structured.

  • Choose I Lost an Item or I Found an Item
  • Fill in title, category, color, and description
  • Specify building, floor, room, and date
  • Optionally upload a photo (image only, max 5 MB)
  • AI automatically generates a caption from your photo

🤖 AI-Powered Match Suggestions

When you submit a report, CampusFind compares it against open items of the opposite type in the same category.

If an uploaded photo exists, the AI-generated caption is used to highlight likely matches. These suggestions appear privately on your dashboard under "Potential AI Matches."

🔐 Claiming & Ownership Verification

CampusFind uses a two-step verification process to protect owners and finders:

  • Step 1 — Finder Confirms: The person who found the item must first confirm the match.
  • Step 2 — Owner Claims: The original reporter then requests the claim.
  • Step 3 — Reporter Approves: Once approved, the item is marked as Claimed.

If rejected, the item returns to Open so others can still view it.

📌 Track Every Item's Status

Every report moves through clear, visible states on your dashboard.

Open — Actively listed

The item has been reported and is visible in public listings. Anyone can browse it.

Pending — Claim in review

A claim has been submitted and is waiting for the reporter to approve or reject it.

Claimed — Owner confirmed

The reporter approved the claim. The item has been returned to its rightful owner.

Resolved — Closed out

Reserved for fully closed cases (included in the data model for future use).

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Do I need an account to browse items?

You can view open items without logging in. However, to report, claim, or upload a photo, you must sign up and log in.

How does the photo upload work?

Photos are optional, must be image files, and are limited to 5 MB. Once uploaded, the AI (BLIP model) generates a caption that helps matching and visibility.

Can I delete my own report?

Yes. From your dashboard, you can delete any report you created at any time.

What happens if my claim is rejected?

The item returns to Open status and remains visible in listings. You may request a claim again if new evidence appears.

Is my photo visible to everyone?

Photos are only returned to the reporter, the claimant, or a matched user — it's not public to all users.

Are there email or push notifications?

Email and push notifications are planned for a future version. Right now, you'll see status updates on your dashboard.
